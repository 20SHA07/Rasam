FAQ

The questions
worth asking.

Does Rasam replace our accounting software?

No. The prototype helps turn invoice details into reviewed rows for Excel. You keep your accounting software and decide how to use the exported entries.

Can it read Arabic and English invoices?

The local app can send invoice PDFs and images to an AI model for Arabic and English field extraction. Results need a human check, especially for blurry images, unusual layouts or unclear numbers. It is not yet benchmarked across GCC invoice types.

Does AI reading work in the online workbench?

The online workbench lets you explore manual entry, review and Excel export. AI reading runs through the local app with your own OpenAI API key. When you click Read with AI there, the document is sent to OpenAI and API usage charges may apply.

Can Rasam learn from multiple GCC invoices?

Yes, that is part of the plan. A useful first step is company-specific memory: store reviewed examples, find a similar past invoice, and suggest the supplier or account choice that was approved before. Corrections would stay separate for each company.

The current prototype does not do this yet, and uploading invoices does not train a model. Further model training is a separate option to evaluate after collecting labeled examples with permission and measuring results on invoices it has not seen.

Is it connected to Business Central or ZATCA?

Not yet. The prototype exports an Excel workbook. Business Central integration, e-invoice QR validation and connections to regional e-invoicing services are planned work, not available features.

What happens to the invoices I upload?

The online prototype keeps its queue in the current browser session. Refreshing or closing the page clears it, so export approved entries before leaving. AI reading in the local app sends the selected document to OpenAI only when you request it.

What does early access look like?

Start with a conversation about your workflow. We want to understand the kinds of invoices you handle, where manual entry slows you down, and what an export must contain to be useful to your team.
